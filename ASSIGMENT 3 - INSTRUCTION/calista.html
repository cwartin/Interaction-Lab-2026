Calista's Poster

Parsons ‘29
BFA Communication Design

For this project, I wanted you to feel the energy I felt throughout the day. I chose blue, lime green, and teal because those were the three colors I kept seeing. Teal was my T-shirt, and lime green is how I remember the wind, which was constant and impossible to ignore. [Add one line about blue here.] The spiky "kiki" shape comes from two things: the cold gusts hitting me all day, and how giddy I felt in spite of them. That mix of sharp and bouncy is what I wanted the poster to carry. I only got four hours of sleep, so the poster has just four abstract shapes, and they fill all of the space. A tired day, but a very full one.
